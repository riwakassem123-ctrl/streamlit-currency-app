Lebanese Exchange rate app 
the streamlit app reflects an overview of the dynamics of the Lebanese exchange rate (fluctuations , depreciation , changes and crisis) 
key features:
year and month filters 
line/area toggle 
depreciation progress bar 
four visualizations (rate over time , monthly fluctuations, yearly average , year over year change)
crisis annotation 
summary statistics 
KPI  cards 
download filtered  data 
the dataset contains 746 rows and 12 columns
